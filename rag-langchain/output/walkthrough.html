Where #1’s score comes from

Multiply the two vectors number by number, then add up all 1,536 products. That sum is the cosine similarity. No single dimension decides it: the score is many small agreements (red) minus disagreements (blue).

sum of products = 0.4162 = cosine similarity → Chroma’s distance = 1 − 0.4162 = 0.5838 (reported: 0.5839)
