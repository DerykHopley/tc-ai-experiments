# Data

`artists.csv` and `releases.csv` are from the [Global Music Popularity & Cultural Attention](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blixture/global-music-popularity-and-cultural-attention) dataset by Blixture on Kaggle, copied unchanged from [rag-langchain/data](../../rag-langchain/data/). They cover 51 artists with their MusicBrainz metadata and releases. They're published under the MIT licence, copied below.

The evaluation questions in `../eval/questions.json` are ours, written with Claude from this data.
